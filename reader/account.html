Log in to save bookmarks

No password, no private account. Choose any unique name or string. Enter exactly the same name on any visit or device to load its bookmarks. Anyone who enters that name can view, add, or remove its bookmarks. Avoid personal information and do not use a password you use elsewhere.

Names are case-sensitive, with surrounding spaces ignored. Use 1–64 visible characters. This browser stays logged in for 30 days. Each IP address gets up to 100 login submissions in a rolling seven-day period; reading and saving bookmarks do not count. Up to 1,000 bookmarks can be saved per name.
